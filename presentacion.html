The 4 Fantastic forces...

4 / 7

Any object in flight is controlled by four forces, let's learn which ones:

  • Thrust: The force that moves the object forward.
  • Drag: The air resistance that slows it down.
  • Weight: The gravity pulling the object to the ground.
  • Lift: The upward force created by the wings against weight.

Wing Geometry and Winglets

5 / 7

How geometry changes flight???

  • Curving the back of the wing upward pushes the tail down, making the nose go up (pitch effect, wow).
  • And folding wing tips upward creates winglets, stopping large whirlpools (vortices) to keep flight stable and save millions in fuel.
